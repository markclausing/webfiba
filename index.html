HOW TO PLAY

  • Shoot — hold, release at the top of the jump. Green is perfect.
  • Dunk — sprint at the rim and shoot inside four metres.
  • Pass — aim with the stick; centred finds the open man.
  • Alley-oop — lob to a team-mate cutting to the rim.
  • Flow — quick passes light the floor and lift your shooting.
  • Clear it — after a rebound or steal, take it behind the arc.
  • Defence — shoot to jump, pass to reach, lob to switch.
